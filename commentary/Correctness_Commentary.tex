	\section{On Suffix Trees}
	A suffix tree is a structure which expands on the suffix trie, and also tries in general.  Almost all modern usages have now been replaced by the suffix array, however the suffix tree is still invaluable in terms of theory.
	It's used in many different scenarios, most notable discovering the full human genome.  Generally, it's an optimisation on all string operations, such as searching, finding recurring substrings, etc.
